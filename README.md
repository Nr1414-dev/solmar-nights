SOLMAR NIGHTS is an open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser: drive and steal cars across the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar, outrun the police and their helicopter through a 5-star wanted system, run seven story missions for fixer Mara Solis, take taxi fares, find 12 hidden packages, and buy your way out of trouble at the gun store, the spray shop and the diner. 2D top-down, keyboard + mouse, vanilla JavaScript with no libraries and no network — the whole city, traffic, pedestrians, police AI, day/night cycle and soundtrack are generated in code.

v2 adds: motorcycles (the fastest, most fragile ride in Solmar), Salt Kings hit squads — armed gang waves that hunt you after your first job, paying a $150 bounty per King and a $200 squad bonus — rain storms that slick the roads and cut every car's grip, and body armor sold at Ironworks Arms that takes hits before your health does.

v2.2 adds: a Settings screen (O key, or from the title and pause menus) with separate music and effects volumes, three minimap sizes (N cycles them in play) and a screen-shake toggle, all saved on the machine; a police helicopter that joins the chase at four stars, circles overhead with a spotlight and a rotor you can hear coming — duck off the road, under cover, to shake it off; fog that rolls in off the bay, hiding the far side of the street and making the Kings shoot worse; a seventh story job, The Last Delivery, running three parcels across the city in 180 seconds in the new courier delivery van parked at the Solmar Courier Depot; Salt Kings Enforcers in every third hit squad (120 HP, faster trigger, $250 bounty, orange on the minimap); pocket-park fountains; and a two-column How to Play that finally fits the screen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```
